b. My Hero: Jesus Christ

Background

Jesus of Nazareth is the central figure of Christianity, believed by Christians to be the Son of God and the Messiah. He lived roughly 2,000 years ago in the region of Judea and Galilee. His life, teachings, death, and resurrection are recorded in the Gospels of the New Testament.

Why Jesus?

I am a religious person and believe in God and Jesus Christ. I believe God sent His only Son, Jesus, to die for our sins (John 3:16). Jesus is my hero because of His incredible love for humanity.

He left His place in heaven to enter a world of suffering and pain (Philippians 2:5–8). Jesus was beaten, mocked, and ultimately crucified (Matthew 27:27–31; Luke 23:33–34). Isaiah 52:14 describes how badly He was beaten, showing the suffering He endured.

He endured this suffering for the very people who called for His death (Luke 23:21). Though innocent, He died a criminal's death (1 Peter 2:22–24). His love is unmatched because He died not only for His friends but for His enemies too (Romans 5:6–8; John 15:13).

Jesus also demonstrated that true greatness is not about power, popularity, or status. Instead, He taught His followers to serve others with humility and love. This is one of the biggest reasons I look up to Him as my hero.

Favourite Quote

"Not so with you. Instead, whoever wants to become great among you must be your servant."
— Jesus Christ, Mark 10:43

I love this verse because in the world, greatness is often defined by power or status. But Jesus made it clear that true leaders serve others, put people first, and lead through humility.

This verse also influences how I want to live my own life. As a student, I believe leadership should not be about being better than everyone else. Instead, it should be about helping others, being respectful, taking responsibility, and using my abilities to make a positive difference.

How Jesus Influences Me

Jesus' example challenges me to be patient with difficult group members, honest even when cheating would be easier, and humble enough to ask for help when I do not understand something. His example reminds me that character matters more than grades or achievements.

His sacrifice also reminds me to show compassion toward people who may not always treat me well. If Jesus was willing to forgive and love those who opposed Him, I believe I should also strive to show forgiveness, kindness, and understanding toward others.

Favourite Scene

My favourite scene is in the Garden of Gethsemane, where Jesus prayed in anguish before His arrest (Luke 22:42–44). He was fully human—suffering, afraid, and asking if there was another way—yet He still chose to obey God's will.

That scene shows me that courage isn't the absence of fear; it's choosing to do what's right while afraid. I carry that lesson into every exam, presentation, difficult assignment, and difficult decision I face.
